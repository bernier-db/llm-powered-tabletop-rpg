# The Red Hand-Print

## Description

A hand-print, palm and all five fingers, burned into the bark of a roadside oak roughly
two hundred meters south of Stonebridge's tree line. The burn is deliberate — scorched in
with something hot and precise, not a wildfire char. The edges are clean. The color left
behind is a deep rust-red that does not look like ordinary fire-marking.

## Placement

- **Where**: `location_south_road`, on the east-facing side of a large oak, at eye height
  for an adult standing on the road.
- **When visible**: any time the party travels south on the road, or if they follow up on
  the carter's report.

## Player-Facing Clue Yield

| Check | DC | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Perception (notice while passing) | 10 | See the mark; note it is deliberate |
| Religion or History (identify) | 12 | Recognize it as the Red Sigil — the condemned symbol from forty years ago |
| Nature (examine the burn) | 14 | The burn is recent — within the last week; not natural fire damage |
| Arcana (detect residue) | 16 | Faint transmutation residue; the burning was ritual, not mundane flame |

## Payoff

When the party successfully identifies this as the Red Sigil (`cult_of_red_sigil`),
the foreshadow seed is considered paid off. The Director should note this and may
choose to have Antagonist A become aware that someone is following the trail.

Beat reference: `01_arrival` — the DM may surface this seed during the arrival beat
if the party asks about the carter's story, or it can be held for their first
southward movement out of the tavern.

## Director Notes

- If the party ignores this seed entirely, the Director should re-surface it
  through Marta's reluctant confession (she saw the brand on the robed figure's hand).
- Do not use both the road mark and Marta's memory simultaneously — pick one
  entry point, reserve the other for reinforcement if the party is slow.
