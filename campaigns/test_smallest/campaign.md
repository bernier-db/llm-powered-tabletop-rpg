# The Quiet Rot of Stonebridge

A small-stakes investigation in a town that has barely noticed it is being hollowed out.
The cult of the red sigil is only weeks old, but it has already claimed one person the party may care about.

## Player-Facing Hooks

- **The Missing Ledger**: A merchant named Vellis has not opened his stall in three days.
  His apprentice found his counting house locked from the inside — with Vellis gone and his ledger missing.
  The market warden is offering a modest reward for either the man or the book.

- **Strange Symbol**: A carter arriving from the forest road described a red hand-print burned into a tree
  at the roadside. No one will say it out loud, but the older residents recognize the shape.
  It matches a symbol condemned by the church forty years ago.

- **The Barkeep's Worry**: Marta at the Drunken Goose has been quietly watering everyone's drinks for two days —
  she cannot sleep. She will not say why without some trust built first. She knows something.

## Scope

One session (~3 hours). No dungeon. Resolution is social + one optional wilderness encounter.
Stakes: one NPC's life, one faction clock advancing one segment.
