# Greyhill Region

## Description

Rolling grey-green hills cut by the Ardith River. The forest presses close on the eastern and northern
flanks; the western approach opens onto the lowland trade road. Settlements are small and inward-facing —
most families have lived here for four or five generations. Strangers get looked at twice.

The church had a strong presence here until the Red Sigil suppression forty years ago, when the diocesan
inquisitor publicly burned a cache of cult materials in the Stonebridge market square. Since then,
church attendance has been high but hollow — ritual performed without conviction.

## Key Settlements

- **Stonebridge** — the largest settlement in the region; a small market town astride the Ardith ford.
  See [`settlement_stonebridge.md`](settlement_stonebridge.md).

## Regional Notes

- Industry: mixed grain farming and timber; modest quarry on the north hill.
- Government: a regional steward appointed by a distant lord; rarely visits.
- Wealth: modest — not poor, not prosperous.
- Disposition baseline: suspicious (toward outsiders), neutral (within community).
- Regional tension: the church's grip has loosened; something is moving into the gap.
