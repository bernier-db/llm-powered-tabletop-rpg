# Stonebridge

## Description

A town of roughly four hundred souls clustered around the old stone bridge that gives it its name.
The bridge is two centuries old — solid, mossy, slightly too narrow for a loaded wagon and a horse
to pass at the same time, which causes a small argument approximately every market day.

The town square is anchored by the church of the Bound Flame (currently served by a single elderly
deacon; the last proper priest left six years ago), a well, a notice board, and the market district.
Side streets run outward in a loose grid of timber-frame buildings.

## Notable Locations Within Settlement

- **The Drunken Goose** — tavern and inn on the north side of the square. See [`location_drunken_goose.md`](location_drunken_goose.md).
- **Market District** — the open stall market on the east side of the square. See [`location_market_district.md`](location_market_district.md).
- **South Road (town exit)** — the road segment leaving Stonebridge southward into the forested hills. See [`location_south_road.md`](location_south_road.md).

## Settlement Facts

- Population: ~400
- Industry: market town; regional grain and timber trade
- Government: market warden (elected annually by the stall holders); deacon for spiritual matters
- Wealth: modest
- Disposition: suspicious of outsiders / neutral within community

## History Note

The church's inquisitor burned cult materials in this square forty years ago. The cobblestones
near the well still carry faint dark staining. Most residents know the story but won't tell it
to strangers.
